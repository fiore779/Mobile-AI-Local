# Mobile AI Local Privacy Policy

Effective date: {{effective date}}　Developer: {{developer name}}　Contact: {{contact email}}

Mobile AI Local ("the app") is an AI chat app that runs entirely on your device. The app does not send your conversations or data to the developer or to any third-party server.

## 1. Information stored on your device
The following is stored only in the app's private storage (not visible to other apps). The developer does not receive it.
- Conversations (your questions, the AI's answers, conversation titles, system prompts)
- AI model files you download
- Settings (theme, language, generation settings, the Wi-Fi-only option, and so on)

## 2. Internet connections
The app connects to the internet only when you do one of the following.
- **Searching for models:** the search words you type are sent to Hugging Face (huggingface.co), the model distribution service.
- **Downloading models:** the files of the model you choose are fetched from Hugging Face.

In these connections, Hugging Face receives your IP address and basic connection information. Hugging Face handles it under its own privacy policy. Your conversations are never part of these connections. The AI generates all answers on your device and needs no connection.

## 3. What we do not collect
The app does not ask you to create an account. It contains no advertising, no usage analytics, no crash reporting, and nothing that tracks you. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

## 4. Permissions
- Internet: to search for and download models
- Network state: to apply the "download only on Wi-Fi" option
- Foreground service: to keep an answer being generated when you leave the app (on Android 13 and later no notification is shown; on Android 12 and earlier a minimal, unobtrusive notification appears)

## 5. Reporting inappropriate output
If an answer is a problem, you can contact the developer with the app's report function. It creates a draft email, containing the answer you are reporting, in your email app. **It reaches the developer only if you send it.**

## 6. Deleting information
- You can delete conversations at any time on the History screen.
- You can delete downloaded models on the Models screen.
- Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.

## 7. About AI output
AI answers can be wrong or inappropriate. Check anything important yourself.

## 8. Children
The app is not primarily directed at children under 13.

## 9. Changes
If we change this policy, we will announce it on this page. If we add a feature that connects to an external AI service in the future, we will show a confirmation screen before any conversation is sent to that provider, and update this policy.
